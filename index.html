About NyayaTrack

NyayaTrack is an initiative of the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India to make the criminal justice system more transparent, accessible and citizen-friendly. It allows tracking of First Information Reports (FIRs) and cases across nationwide connected police jurisdictions.
